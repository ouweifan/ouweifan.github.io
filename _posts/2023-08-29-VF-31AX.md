This is the Soul Web Shop release of Mirage's VF-31AX custom. I used it as a quick post-processing practice piece, with the finished shot meant to sit nicely as a desktop wallpaper. The look loosely references the kind of finish often seen in Hobby Japan: a cooler-toned starfield background, paired with a small amount of directional motion blur to give the image a sense of speed.
